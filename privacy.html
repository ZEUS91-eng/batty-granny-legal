Privacy Policy

Batty Granny, by Xcong. Effective date: 13 November 2026.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you play the Batty Granny game for mobile devices (the "Game"), published by Xcong ("we", "us"), how it is used and the choices you have.

1. Summary

  • The Game has no accounts, and we do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your game progress (levels, coins, settings) is stored only on your device.
  • We do not run our own servers that collect your data. The Game uses Google and Unity services for advertising and Google Play for purchases, and these services collect some information as described below.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information collected by the services in the Game

The following information may be collected automatically by the third-party services listed in section 5:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.
  • Approximate location, worked out from your IP address (not GPS).
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, screen size and app version.
  • App activity, such as which ads were shown, ad views and taps, and when the Game was opened.
  • Diagnostics, such as crash logs and performance data.
  • Purchase history, meaning whether a purchase was made in the Game (see section 4).

3. How the information is used

  • To show ads in the Game, including personalized ads where you allow them, and to measure how ads perform.
  • To give you rewards for watching optional rewarded video ads.
  • To deliver and restore your purchases.
  • To prevent fraud, such as fake ad clicks.
  • To find and fix crashes and improve the Game.

4. In-app purchases

The Game offers optional in-app purchases: Remove Ads and coin packs. All payments are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card or bank details. Google Play shares only the purchase confirmation with the Game, so your items can be delivered and Remove Ads can be restored after a reinstall. Advertising partners such as Unity Ads may receive information that a purchase happened, to measure ad performance, as described in their privacy policies.

5. Third-party services

These services have their own privacy policies, which explain what they collect and how they use it:

  • Google AdMob (ads, including ad mediation): policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Unity Ads (ads shown through AdMob mediation): unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Google Play Services and Google Play Billing (app services and purchases): policies.google.com/privacy

6. Your ad choices

  • Europe (EEA), UK and Switzerland: when you first open the Game you are asked whether you agree to personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy button in the Game's main menu.
  • United States: where your state's privacy law applies, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your information for targeted ads with the Privacy button in the Game's main menu, where shown.
  • Any country: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

If you do not allow personalized ads, you still see ads, but they are less relevant to you.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or limit its use, to withdraw consent and to not be treated differently for using these rights. Because we do not hold your data on our own servers, most requests are handled by the services listed in section 5, and we will help you contact them. You can also contact us at the email below. You can stop all collection at any time by uninstalling the Game; this deletes your game progress from your device.

8. Data retention

Your game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the Game or clear its data. Information collected by third-party services is kept according to their own privacy policies.

9. International transfers

The services in section 5 may process information in countries other than yours, including outside the European Economic Area. Where the law requires it, they use legal safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (or a higher age where required by local law), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

11. Security

We use the security protections of Google Play and the Android platform, and our service providers use encryption to protect information in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when the Game adds new features or services. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and change the effective date at the top.

13. Contact
